## Scientific Studies (Evidence-Based Content)
Weight Management: Studies showing legume intake correlates with lower BMI.
Longevity: The "Blue Zones" connection (legumes as a common denominator).
Disease Prevention: Clinical trials on beans and Type 2 Diabetes management.

## Types of Beans (Nutritional Profiles)
Black Beans: High in anthocyanins (antioxidants).
Chickpeas (Garbanzo): Rich in manganese and folate.[2][3]
Lentils: Iron-dense and fastest to cook.
Kidney Beans: Highest in fiber and complex carbohydrates.
Soy (Edamame/Tempeh): Complete protein profile.

## Meal Prep Strategies
Modular/Building Block Prep: Cooking GGB components separately to mix-and-match.
Batch Cooking: Preparing 1-2 large recipes (stews/chilis) for the whole week.
Cascade Cooking: Using leftovers from Monday’s dinner as the base for Tuesday’s lunch.
The 1-Hour Sprint: How to prep three different meals in 60 minutes.
