## Business goals
Generate income by selling digital products that help busy people to eat healthy vegan food according to the GGB method.
We enable people to prepare food at home by providing easy recipes in simple to follow meal plans.

**KPIs:**
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR): The total predictable revenue generated by your meal plan subscribers each month.
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): How much you’re spending on ads/marketing to get one new subscriber (Life Time Value). (Aim for a 3:1 LTV to CAC ratio).
- Social Proof Velocity: The number of tagged "meal prep" photos or positive reviews generated per week.

### Customer Retention & Value
**Primary Goal:** Keep customers cooking (and paying) for at least 6 months (that would mean 6x4 meal plans).
**KPIs:**
- Churn Rate: The percentage of subscribers who cancel each month. (Healthy targets are usually under 5-7%).
- Average Order Value (AOV): If you sell add-ons (like specialized grocery lists, protein powder recommendations, or prep videos), this tracks the average spend.
- NPS (Net Promoter Score): A simple survey asking: "How likely are you to recommend our GGB plans to a friend?"

### Product Excellence & "The Busy Factor"
**Primary Goal:** Maximize user success and minimize "time-to-table."
**KPIs:**
- Average Prep Time: Tracking user feedback to ensure recipes stay within the promised window (e.g., under 30 minutes).
- Ingredient Overlap Percentage: A high overlap (using the same grain/bean across multiple meals) reduces waste—a huge win for busy users.

### KPI Summary
**Financial:** MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue),Ensures the business is sustainable and predictable.
**Loyalty:** Churn Rate,Tells you if the meals are actually delicious and easy enough to keep doing.
**Efficiency:** Time-to-Table,"Validates your promise to ""busy people."""
**Marketing:** LTV (Lifetime Value),Tells you how much a customer is worth over the total time they stay with you.

- Demographics: 20-42, urban/suburban, European, mixed gender, middle-to-upper income, English as a second language, health-conscious, time-poor professional, bit of a body-hacker mentality.

## User Journey
### 1. Discovery: The "Anti-Pizza Day" Hook
Instead of "fast food," we market "Predictable Excellence." These should be pushed as ads on social media.
**OPTION 1 FOR DINNERS**
- A Reel showing the transition from a stressful office exit to a calm, 15-minute re-heating of a gourmet Chili Sin Carne with fresh avocado and nachos.
The Message: "The grocery shopping is done. The plan is set. Your only job is to enjoy it."
The Goal: Acknowledge the European appreciation for real food while solving the "arriving home hungry" pain point.
**OPTION 2 FOR LUNCHES**
The Hook (Reel): A split screen. Left side: Someone staring at a delivery app for 15 minutes ($25 spent). Right side: Heating Fejao and rice in microwave, cook fresh broccoli, cook quinoa and prep a bowl for lunch next day, open a bag of pre-washed greens, rinse a tin of chickpeas, slice a cucumber, express a lemon, grate red cabbage (all in 15 min).
The Reality Check: "Cook with a plan. Two times 15-Minute GGB Desk Lunch."
The Message: You don’t need a chef's knife or a 6-burner stove. You need a shopping list that works like LEGOs. 
Message: less packaging, fresher food. 
Variations of this with other meals.
**OPTION 3 for Lunch**
Split screen with stressful shopping (queuing people) a soggy salad for 4€ during lunchbreak, versus opening a fresh looking home-made bowl (4x for 4 Euro) on a park bench near the office.
**Discovery Posts**
- Template Brief: Short-form hooks, value proposition, relevant hashtags
- Sample Heading (EN): "5 Vegan Staples for Quick GGB Bowls"; Outline: 1. Hook 2. Why it matters 3. Quick tips 4. CTA

### 2. Lead Magnet: The "Decision-Free Monday"
They need to experience how the plan removes the 6:00 PM panic.
**The Freebie:** 
**OPTION 1 Free GGB starter week with a discount code for Gumroad via PM**
They get: GGB Starter Week plan pdf + epub
You get: a PM/DM on social media -> target group info
The Experience: You provide the exact European-market shopping list (grams/metric) for that one meal.
The Logic: They see that by cooking once on Monday, they've already secured their Saturday dinner through smart freezing and so on
**OPTION 2 subscribe to substack and receive the plan by 8 emails a as free content**
Then continue with teaser mails for the next week with plan behind paywall
**Lead MAgnet Information Phase**
- Template Brief: In-depth recipe or meal-plan detail; FAQ; substitutions
- - Sample Heading (EN): "Cook 1x for 2 meals – Recipe Details"; Outline: ingredients, steps, nutrition, substitutions
- Sample Heading (EN): "GGB 15-Min Vegan Power Bowl – Recipe Details"; Outline: ingredients, steps, nutrition, substitutions

### 3. Conversion: The 3-2-6 Strategy
Now you introduce the 2 week Plan as their new weekly operating system.
The USP: "3 cooking sessions = 6 days of high-quality dinners" + 1 week dinner + lunch (3 new dinner recipes + 3 lunch recipes)
The Tone: Scientific and structured. Emphasize the GGB Philosophy (Grains, Greens, Beans) as a nutritional framework that fuels their professional life without the bloat of junk food.
European Localization: Ensure the interface is optimized for European users (metric system, ingredients found in local shops like Lidl, Carrefour, or Mercadona).
**Product Page**
- Template Brief: Gumroad product page, pricing, value props, FAQs, delivery format
- Sample Heading (EN): "GGB Starter 2-Weeks Vegan Meal Plan – Buy Now"; Outline: benefits, what's included, pricing, CTA

### 4. Retention: The "Culinary Level-Up"
Keep them engaged by honoring their appreciation for food quality.
Optimization Tips: Send them the "Pro Tips" from your guide—like using bicarb (soda) to ensure soft beans or how to make their own vegetable stock from the cooking water.
The "Freshness" Guarantee: Remind them that they aren't eating 5-day-old fridge food; they are eating freshly thawed meals or "cook-from-scratch" 25-minute recipes like the Spicy Sweet Potatoes.
**Retention/Advocacy Posts** 
- Template Brief: Newsletter/Community post; referral rewards; user-generated content prompts
- Sample Heading (EN): "VIP: 10% off next plan + exclusive recipes"; Outline: benefits, how to claim

## Content marketing strategy

### Pillars
- Plant-Powered Living: vegan nutrition, macro balance
- Vegan GGB Meal Prep System: no-brainer, batch-cook, cost-/time-per-meal
- Sustainability & Ethics: waste-reduction, no meat
- Education & Community: recipes, plans, surveys, challenges

### Channels & Cadence
- Facebook: posts, carousels, short videos; 3-5 posts/week
- Instagram: Reels (shorts), carousels, stories; 5 posts/week
- Email/Newsletter: email sequences on sign up that should lead to paywall subscription (substack or other), newsletter outside the platform for product updates and for blog if not subscribed yet
- Blog: with CTA, serve as articles on substack (where plans are included)
and can feed posts
### Funnel Logic
- Discovery: Reels, carousels, blog-like posts; broad interest
- Information: Detailed recipe pages; description of meal plans and cooking strategies; bite-sized how-tos of meal plan strategies
- Transaction: Gumroad meal plans, PDFs/EPUBs; Amazon Months Plans
- Retention: Newsletter, Substack

### KPI mapping
- Reach/Impressions, Engagement rate, Follower growth
- Click-through rate to Gumroad, Conversion rate (purchase)
- Average order value, Repeat purchases, Churn rate
- Email open rate, click-to-open rate, unsubscribe rate

### Governance & Cadence
- Editorial calendar: 4-week sprint cadence; weekly planning; monthly review
- Localization QA: bilingual checks; glossary alignment; tone parity
- Brand guardrails: tone, content safety, medical claims caution
