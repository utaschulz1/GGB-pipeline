## naming
- folders with dashes 
- files with underscores
- front name: Vegan GGB Meal Prep

## GGB
- GGB stands for Grains Greens Beans or from a grain, a green, a bean
- GGB capitalized
- We are loose about "greens", it can be any vegetable, no matter the colour. Though, anything green with every meal is important to us. 
- A grain is food from grain like bread or rice or barley as well as high-carb sides, like sweet-potatoes, taco, wraps, nachos, potatoes.

## Recipes
- Keep it short, dont mention washing and cleaning the vegetables or peeling the onions or shaking the cilantro or salat. This is not a cooking school. Instead, a step starts with cutting or grading or similar...
- Rice is just "cook the rice according to instructions on the package" dont go into detail

## Headings
- Title case (CPC)
- HTML title (even if not used in the initial phase): Best practices for title tag length say that your title should no more than 60-80 characters. An important number to keep in mind is Google's maximum title threshold of around 65 characters. Anything longer and your title starts to get truncated.

## Meta description
The current best practice as of June 2018 is to keep the description under 160 characters.

## Brand & Voice & Readability
- Voice: warm, practical, non-judgmental; concise steps; supportive
- Terminology: consistent vegan terms; 
- **avoid medical claims; **
- avoid cultural stereotypes
- Avoid political content; respect cultural differences
- Ensure accessibility (alt text, readable contrast)
- Avoid matrimonial or sensitive demographic stereotypes
- Write European English for second language speaker
- Do not create tables unless provided in template

## Localization: 
English is baseline; 
write translatable: 
- avoid hard-to-translate expressions
- don't use bullet points within a sentence
- don't use plural(s)

## Units
All units are metric units.
Currency in Euro.
