Meal plan considered per week with 6 days of planed meals
Below, weight of grains and legumes are considered in  cooked state
## 1. Decide the scope
dinner, dinner+lunch, dinner&lunch...
## 2. Decide the difficulty
no cognitive complexity: cook one recipe at the time, easy recipes, little to handle in parallel
more cognitive effort: batch cook for more than one recipe at once, handle several thing in parallel
## 3. Decide on equipment and theme
This will influence the strategies that can be applied.
## 4. Decide the strategy
Strategy depends on difficulty and equipement (freezer or not, big pan, oven...)
batch cook, batch cook basics and combine with different sides, cook 1x for 2x, cook every time fresh but fast...
## 5. Choose main ingredient legumes (depends on theme)
Rule of thumb for 2 people for dinner+lunch 1200g of legumes A, 1200g of legumes B, 600g of legumes C.
Example 1200g chickpeas, 1200g white beans, 600g peas
Half for dinner only.
Don't overdo one type of legumes in a week, unless it's in the theme. Consider, if it is managable to cook the amount in a standard slowcooker.
## 6. Search for dishes, combine or invent
Rule of thumb per serving dinner (lunch at least 75%):
**Calories**
450-800 kcal
Add salad or fruit as desert when low calory!
Add nuts as snack/desert/into the rice or as topping when low calories.
**Combine**
- Always add a salad if the vegetables are fully cooked. Less vegetables, more salad.
- No legumes in the main dish? Make a simple bean salad. Ideal for pasta with red sauce: just add black eyed beed salad with bell pepper. Done in 15 min.
**Macros**
per serving:
100-200g legumes
200g grains
200-400g vegetables
total: 600-800g food + liquid
consider the calories of the sauce (watery vs cocosmilk) and presence of high-calory food like nuts, humus...
