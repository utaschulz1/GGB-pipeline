4 servings
Ingredients:
250g Quinoa real
50g roasted almonds without salt
50g Korinthen (alternative: dried apricots, chopped)
juice of 1 Orange
1 EL Orangenblütenwasser (optional)
400g chickpeas
800g carrots
30g Kürbiskerne
juice of 1 lemon
1 TL Honey
2 EL Sesamöl
4 EL Olivenöl
1 Bund Koriander (alternative: Petersilie, Minze)
Salz, Pfeffer, Zimt

1. Prepare the quinoa according to the package 
2. Peel and grate the carrots and put them into a large bowl.
3. Rinse the chickpeas (if canned) and add them in the bowl.
4. In a cup, mix lemon juice, olive oil, sesam oil, honey, salt, peper and Zimt.
5. Add the dressing to the bowl and stir well. Season to taste.
6. Add the Kürbiskerne and Korinthen and stir.
7. Lange Stiele vom Korianer entfernen, Rest grob hacken und in einem kleine Behälter separat aufbewahren.
8. When the quinoa is cooked, mix it with the roasted almonds.
9. Distribute quinoa and salad into 4 lunch containers and sprinkle the Korianer on top of the quinoa. Refridgerate.
