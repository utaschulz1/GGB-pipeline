Ultrafast, easy, inexpensive
15min
4 servings
300g couscous integral
400g Kichererbsen
1/2 bund Minze (alt. Petersilie)
100g Rucola
50g roasted almonds
1 Zitrone
1 limette
30g Kürbiskerne (alt. Sonnenblumenkerne)
1 EL Olivenöl
200g große Tomaten (2große)
1 Salatgurke 200g
Salz, Pfeffer

1. Bring 400ml of water to boil.
2. Mix almonds and couscous in a pan.
3. Add the boiling water and let it soak with the lid closed.
4. Rinse the Chickpeas and put them into a bowl
5. Wash the limette and zest it, add the zest to the chickpeas
6. Add lemonjuice and limette juice, salt and pepper and stir
7. Chop the tomato and cucumber adn add it to the chickpeas
8. add the olive oil and stir
9. Blatter von der Minze abzupfen und grob hacken. Zum Salat dazugeben und umrühren.
10. Rucola grob schneiden und zum Salat geben.
11. Stir the couscous and taste if it is done. In case it is to dry, add some more boiling water.
12. Distribute into 4 lunchboxes keeping couscous and salad as separate as possible. Refridgerate.
