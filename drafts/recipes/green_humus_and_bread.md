4 servings

500g bread
200g Gemüsesticks (cucumber 100g, celery 100g)
400g chickpeas
1/2 lemon (juice)
1/2 bund Minze
200ml Brühe
50ml Öl
50g Tahini (optional)
2 TL cumin, Salz, chili
1 bund Petersilie
200g Erbsen (TK)

Making Humus
General notes: 
- Humus is easier to make in a food processor, but it also works with a hand blender. If using a hand blender, use a bowl to blend the ingredients, not a high jar as you would normally use.
- Start with blending chickpeas and boullion. As soon as you add any Tahini or oil, the mixture binds and gets sticky.

Zubereitung:

1. In a pan, add boiling water to the green peas and bring to boil. Switch off as soon as it boils and drain in a strainer.
2. Pick the leaves of the mint.
3. Add half of the bullion and all of the lemon juice into the food processor or bowl (hand blender).
4. Add half of the chickpeas, mint leaves, cumin, a pinch of salt and chili. Start blending.
5. While blending add chickpeas and more boullion if necessary. The mixture should be like maionese.
6. When all chickpeas are blended, while blending, add Tahini and oil and let run until absorbed.
7. Season with salt and chili to taste.
8. Distribute the Humus over the lunch boxes.
9. Distribute the green peas on top.
10. Petersilie grob hacken und darüber verteilen.
11. Gemüsesticks (Gurke, Stangensellerie) opendrauf legen.
12. Brot separat mitnehmen.
