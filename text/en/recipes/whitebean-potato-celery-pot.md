# White Bean Potato Celery Pot

## Ingredients

## Why this dish

This hearty pot is packed with protein and fiber from white beans, while potatoes and vegetables add substance and comfort. It's a one-pot meal that’s easy to scale up for meal prep. To complete the GGB method, serve with a slice of whole grain bread or a side of brown rice for the grain component.

## Instructions

![Steps White Bean Potato Celery Pot](images/whitebean-potato-celery-pot/steps_grid.jpg)

1. Peel and cube the potatoes; set aside.
2. Cube the zucchini and slice the celery.
3. Chop the onion and mince the garlic.
4. Heat olive oil in a large pot; add oregano, salt, and pepper, then sauté onion and garlic until fragrant.
5. Add zucchini and celery, cook for 5 minutes.
6. Pour in vegetable broth and bring to a boil.
7. Add potatoes and cooked white beans; simmer until potatoes are tender, about 20 minutes.
8. Stir in lemon juice, adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
9. Serve hot, optionally with a slice of whole grain bread.
