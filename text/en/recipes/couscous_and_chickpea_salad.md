# Couscous & Chickpea Salad

## Why this dish
This salad nails **Beans** and **Greens** in under 15 minutes. Chickpeas and couscous make it genuinely filling, while the lemon-lime dressing keeps it bright and fresh.

## Instructions

![Steps Couscous & Chickpea Salad](images/couscous-and-chickpea-salad/steps_grid.jpg)

1. Bring 400ml of water to a boil.
2. If using almonds, combine them with the couscous in a heatproof bowl or pot; otherwise just add the couscous.
3. Pour the boiling water over the couscous, cover with a lid, and let it soak for 5 minutes.
4. Rinse the chickpeas and drain well, then add them to a large bowl.
5. Wash the lime, zest it finely, and add the zest to the chickpeas.
6. Squeeze both the lemon and lime and add the juice to the bowl. Season with salt and pepper and stir.
7. Chop the tomatoes and cucumber into rough chunks and add them to the chickpeas.
8. Add the olive oil and pumpkin seeds and stir.
9. Pick the mint leaves, roughly chop them, and fold into the salad.
10. Roughly chop the arugula and add it to the bowl.
11. Fluff the couscous and taste — if it is too dry, add a splash more boiling water.
12. Distribute into 4 lunchboxes, keeping the couscous and salad as separate as possible. Refrigerate.
