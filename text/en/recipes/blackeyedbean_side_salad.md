# Black-Eyed Bean Side Salad

## Ingredients

<table class="shopping-list">
<tbody>
<tr><td>400g</td><td>Cooked black-eyed beans (canned)</td></tr>
<tr><td>300g</td><td>Green bell pepper (approx. 1 large)</td></tr>
<tr><td>20g</td><td>onion</td></tr>
<tr><td>1 clove</td><td>Garlic</td></tr>
<tr><td>1</td><td>Lemon (zest and juice)</td></tr>
<tr><td>1 tbsp</td><td>Olive oil</td></tr>
<tr><td></td><td>Salt, pepper</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

## Why this dish
A pure **Bean** side that takes 10 minutes and goes with almost anything. Black-eyed beans are mild enough to let the lemon-garlic dressing do the talking. Make a batch and it keeps well in the fridge — or freeze portions for later.

## Instructions

![Steps Black-Eyed Bean Side Salad](images/blackeyedbean_side_salad/steps_grid.jpg)

1. Rinse the black-eyed beans and drain well, then add them to a large bowl.
2. Chop the bell pepper into small pieces and add to the beans.
3. Finely dice the onion and garlic and add them.
4. Zest the lemon finely and add the zest to the bowl.
5. Squeeze the lemon and add the juice.
6. Add the olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and stir well.
