## Why this dish

This vibrant green hummus is packed with plant-based protein from chickpeas and fresh herbs, while the veggie sticks provide crunch and freshness. It's a perfect GGB lunch that's easy to prepare ahead and pack for work or school. The hummus contributes the bean component, the bread provides grains, and the veggie sticks add the greens.

## Instructions

**About making hummus:** A food processor works if it's a powerful one — otherwise a hand blender in a wide bowl gives better results (avoid a tall jar). Start by blending the chickpeas, spices, and broth first. Only add tahini and oil after, as they bind the mixture and make it sticky.

![Steps Green Hummus with Veggie Sticks and Bread](images/green-humus-and-bread/steps_grid.jpg)

1. Add boiling water to the frozen peas in a pan and bring back to a boil. Switch off immediately and drain.
2. Pick the mint leaves.
3. Add half the vegetable broth and all the lemon juice into the food processor or bowl.
4. Add half the chickpeas, mint leaves, cumin, a pinch of salt and chili. Start blending.
5. While blending, gradually add the remaining chickpeas and more broth if needed — the mixture should reach a mayonnaise-like consistency.
6. With the blender still running, add the tahini and olive oil and blend until fully absorbed.
7. Season with salt and chili to taste.
8. Divide the hummus into 4 lunch containers.
9. Distribute the green peas on top.
10. Roughly chop the parsley and scatter over the top.
11. Place the cucumber and celery sticks alongside.
12. Pack the bread separately.
