## Why this dish

This salad is a GGB triple-hitter: the white beans are your **Bean**, the avocado-pea cream adds **Green**, and the sweet potato brings satisfying starchy bulk as a whole-food base. It's naturally vibrant, filling, and holds up beautifully in the fridge — making it one of the best meal-prep lunches in the GGB starter week. Prep once, eat four days.

## Instructions

![Steps Sweet Potato and White Bean Salad](images/sweetpotato-and-whitebean-salad/steps_grid.jpg)

1. **Cook the sweet potatoes.** Choose your method: microwave (most efficient) — place under a lid at 750W for 15 mins; pan — peel, cut into large chunks, bring to a boil in cold water and cook for 5 mins; oven — bake whole at 200°C for 60 mins (good if you're baking anyway).
2. **Rinse the white beans** under cold water and drain well. Transfer to a large bowl.
3. **Dice the red bell pepper** into small pieces and add to the bowl.
4. **Finely chop the onion and garlic** and add to the bowl.
5. **Zest the lemon** directly into the bowl. Then juice both the lemon and lime and add the juice.
6. **Chop the parsley** finely and add to the salad. Mix everything well and season with salt and pepper.
7. **Make the avocado-pea cream:** pour boiling water over the frozen peas, drain immediately, and add to a bowl. Halve the avocados and scoop the flesh in. Blend with a hand blender or food processor until smooth. Season with salt.
8. **Assemble lunchboxes:** divide the sweet potatoes between 4 containers, add a portion of white bean salad, and top with a dollop of avocado-pea cream.
