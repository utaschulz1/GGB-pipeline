## Why this dish

This vibrant salad is a perfect side dish that balances the **Grain**, **Green**, and **Bean** pillars of the GGB meal‑prep philosophy. The crunchy cabbage and fresh parsley provide greens, while pumpkin seeds add protein‑rich nuts and dried apricots contribute natural sweetness—ideal for a quick, nutritious prep that stays crisp even after freezing.

## Instructions

![Steps Red Cabbage Salad](images/red-cabbage-salad/steps_grid.jpg)

1. **Prepare the cabbage.** Place the shredded red cabbage in a large bowl.  
2. **Season.** Add vinegar, salt, and pepper, and massage the mixture for 5 minutes until the volume noticeably decreases.  
3. **Add fruit and nuts.** While the cabbage rests, chop the apple into bite‑sized pieces and cut the dried apricots into halves. Add both to the bowl, then stir in the pumpkin seeds.  
4. **Dress.** Pour in the freshly squeezed orange and lemon juice, mixing until evenly coated.  
5. **Finish.** Roughly chop the parsley and fold it into the salad.  
6. **Rest.** Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (or overnight) to allow flavors to meld. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
