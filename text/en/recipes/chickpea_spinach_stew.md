## Why this dish

This hearty stew hits all three GGB pillars: grains (rice), greens (spinach), and beans (chickpeas). The coconut milk adds richness while keeping it plant-based and satisfying. It's perfect for meal prep—stews often taste better the next day, and the rice can be cooked fresh when reheating.

## Instructions

![Steps Chickpea Spinach Stew](images/chickpea-spinach-stew/steps_grid.jpg)

1. Prepare the rice according to package instructions.
2. Chop the onion, garlic, and ginger.
3. Heat the olive oil in a pot with salt and pepper, add the chopped onion, minced garlic, and grated ginger; cook for 5 minutes.
4. Add the chopped sun-dried tomatoes to the pan.
5. Add the lemon zest and stir.
6. Stir in the cooked chickpeas and cook over medium heat for a few minutes until golden and coated.
7. Pour in the coconut milk and stir.
8. Add the spinach in batches, stirring until wilted.
9. Turn off the heat, stir in the sliced spring onions and a splash of lemon juice.
10. Serve over rice with orange segments on the side. (Alternative: serve with apple slices instead of orange.)
